# weds.live — Marketing Talking Points

## Core Value Props (Lead with These)

### 1. Cost Anchor
> **"Printed cards: ₹15-30 × 500 guests = ₹7,500-15,000 + courier + reprints for mistakes.  
> Digital: ₹599 once. You save ₹10,000+ — that's your honeymoon upgrade."**

### 2. Guest Reality
> **"80% of your guests are on WhatsApp. They'll open a link instantly.  
> Printed cards: lost in mail, thrown away, or 'I'll check later' → never RSVPs."**

### 3. RSVP That Actually Works
> **"Printed: you call 200 people, chase WhatsApp replies, still don't know final count.  
> Digital: one dashboard, real-time 'Yes/No', meal counts, export to Excel for caterer."**

### 4. Last-Minute Changes
> **"Venue changed? Time shifted?  
> Printed: reprint ₹5,000 + panic.  
> Digital: edit in 30 seconds, every guest sees updated info instantly."**

### 5. NRI / Out-of-Town Guests
> **"Your cousins in US/UK/Dubai get the link same day. No international courier ₹2,000, no 3-week wait. They RSVP from phone."**

### 6. Photos & Memories
> **"Printed card = one photo. Digital = unlimited gallery (engagement, pre-wedding, childhood). Guests swipe through your story. Becomes a keepsake they revisit."**

### 7. Eco / Modern
> **"Zero paper waste. Your generation values sustainability. Plus it looks *premium* — not 'cheap digital', but 'smart modern'."**

### 8. QR on Printed Card (Hybrid Close)
> **"Want to keep tradition? Print **one** beautiful card per family with a QR code. ₹500 for 50 premium cards vs ₹10,000 for 500. Best of both."**

## Objection Handlers

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| *"Elders won't know how to open"* | *"We add `?to=Uncle%20Ramesh` → opens with 'Dear Uncle Ramesh'. One tap on WhatsApp. We tested with 70+ year olds — works."* |
| *"Feels less personal"* | *"Your photo, your message, your colors, your voice note (coming). More personal than a mass-printed template."* |
| *"What if link breaks / site down?"* | *"Hosted on GitHub Pages / Vercel — 99.99% uptime. Link works offline once opened (cached). QR scans forever."* |
| *"We want a keepsake"* | *"Download the invitation as PDF. Print one for your album. Digital for guests."* |

## Competitor Positioning

| Competitor | Price | Gap |
|------------|-------|-----|
| Printed cards (local) | ₹7,500-15,000 | 10-25× our price |
| WedMeGood | ₹2,999+ | Full planning suite, not just invitations |
| PaperlessPost / Greenvelope | $15-50 (₹1,200-4,000) | US-focused, expensive for India |
| Canva / Whimsical | Free | No RSVP, no QR, no link, no dashboard |
| Custom dev | ₹15,000-50,000 | Overkill, slow |

**Our sweet spot**: ₹599 — complete digital invitation (link + QR + RSVP + gallery + countdown + map) at 1/10th printed cost.
